The Keto Diet: Is It Right for Athletes?

February 20, 2026 by CatPower Team

The ketogenic diet (keto) is a low-carb, high-fat nutritional approach designed to shift your body into ketosis — a metabolic state where fat becomes the primary energy source instead of carbohydrates.

For gym members aiming to lose body fat while maintaining muscle, keto can be effective when properly structured. However, hydration and electrolyte balance are essential.
